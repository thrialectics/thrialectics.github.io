## How It Works

Answer three questions while thinking about a fictive version of you (this is your beta tester identity). The tool will use semantic embeddings to match your responses to an **Alias** and **Cryptonym** from a database of vectorized names.

Then it generates a 12-word recovery phrase and creates your Ed25519 keypair. Your public key gets registered in the directory; your private key is yours alone.

In the coming weeks after your registration, I’ll give you a public guestbook to sign with your private key.

If you claim an Alias/Cryptonym now and keep your private key safe, you’ll be able to sign future actions with this identity. Your authorship will be cryptographically verifiable, and no one else should be able to forge your signature.

This is beta testing for **Serpent/Dove**, my “magical warfare” alternate reality game launching in Spring 2026. Since we’re in beta:

- The tool may have rough edges
    
- Your feedback will shape the final implementation
    
- Early participants will influence the game design
